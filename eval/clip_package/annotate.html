Is this person with the camera wearer?

Each clip is filmed by a camera worn by a person. One face has a yellow box. Turn the sound on. Decide whether that person is an interaction partner or a bystander. Judge only what happens in the clip.

  • Interaction partner: part of the camera wearer's group or conversation. This includes someone talking to the camera wearer and someone who is with them but not speaking right now, such as a friend at the same table, playing the same game, or listening.
  • Bystander: not part of the camera wearer's group, such as a stranger walking past, someone in the background, or someone in a different group.
